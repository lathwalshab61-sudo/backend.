The Investigator's Archive

Explore landmark cases and foundational practices in friction ridge analysis.

Historical

The Stratton Brothers Case (1905)

Beginner

A foundational case in British legal history. This module explores the first time fingerprint evidence was successfully used to secure a conviction in a murder trial in the UK, highlighting early comparison techniques.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • First use of fingerprints in UK courts
  • Basic ridge characteristic identification
Landmark

The People v. Jennings (1911)

Advanced

The seminal US court case that established the scientific admissibility of fingerprint evidence. Students will review the original latent prints recovered from wet paint and the subsequent expert testimony.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Admissibility standards in US Courts
  • Analysis of prints on complex substrates
Laboratory Practice

Cyanoacrylate Fuming Techniques

Intermediate

A modern procedural guide on the development of latent prints on non-porous surfaces using superglue fuming. Includes visual comparisons of over-fumed vs. optimally developed ridge detail.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Chemical development procedures
  • Identifying processing artifacts
